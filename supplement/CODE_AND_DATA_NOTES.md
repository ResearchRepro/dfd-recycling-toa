# Availability and provenance

Source query: The Lens, 2026-09-01. The 3,291 retrieval records were normalized to 3,128 usable simple patent families. Title/abstract texts originated from the Lens export; non-English texts flagged by Python language detection were machine-translated with Google Translate. Original Lens records, translated abstracts and reviewer annotations are not redistributed in this public update.

Methodology: PatentSBERTa, UMAP, HDBSCAN, CPC class evidence, action–object extraction and semantic function screening. Comparisons with Kim et al. (2026) describe methodological design, not a matched benchmark.

Audit: CSVs under results/revision_validation summarize published run and parameter variations; they do not automatically recompute embeddings from the Lens corpus. Public republication remains subject to institutional and data-provider terms.
